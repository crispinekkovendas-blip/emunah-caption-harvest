160	I fulfilled mitzvot, I overcame the evil inclination, I stood up to trials, I did the will of HaShem, I gave tzedakah, I prayed. A thought can come: "I deserve it."	Cumpri mitsvot, venci o mau impulso, resisti às provas, fiz a vontade de HaShem, dei tsedaká, rezei. Pode vir o pensamento: "eu mereço".
